    // 01 — Mission Briefing

    A cave. A weekend. A box of scraps.

    That's how the first suit got built. No lab, no team of engineers, no second chances. MARK I is the same brief for you: one day to turn an idea into a working prototype that a room full of judges, mentors and recruiters can actually touch.

    Hosted by the GeeksforGeeks Student Chapter at Bennett University, Greater Noida, MARK I brings together 500 builders across AI, web, hardware and open innovation. Come with a team or get matched at check-in. Leave with a demo, a network and, if you're good, a very large cheque.

    // 03 — Mission Log

    24 hours, minute by minute.

    The clock starts at 11:00 on Saturday and does not stop. Mentors rotate through the floor twice; the second round happens at 4 AM for a reason.

    Day 01 Saturday, 14 Nov
    09:00

    Check-in & badge sync

    Collect your recruit badge, claim a table and meet your team. Solo hackers get matched here.

    10:00

    Opening keynote: Suit Up

    Tracks revealed, judging criteria walked through, rules of engagement set.

    11:00

    Hacking begins. MARK I clock starts.

    24:00:00 on the wall. Every workstation, mentor and coffee machine is live.

    15:00

    Mentor round I

    Industry mentors rotate through the floor. Validate your idea before you're too deep to turn back.

    20:00

    Dinner & Expo lightning talks

    Ten minutes, one slide, one sponsor. Then back to the bench.

    00:00

    Midnight fuel drop

    Snacks, energy drinks and the first bugs that only appear after midnight.

    Day 02 Sunday, 15 Nov
    04:00

    Mentor round II: the cave hours

    The floor is quiet and the good ideas are strange. Mentors help you cut scope and ship.

    10:00

    Hands off keyboards

    Submissions lock on the portal. Repo, demo video and a two-minute pitch.

    11:00

    Demos & judging

    Science-fair style. Judges walk the floor; every team demos live at their table.

    14:00

    Closing ceremony & MARK I Champion

    Track winners, podium, cheques and the Mark I trophy. Then sleep.
