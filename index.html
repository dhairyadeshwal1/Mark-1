    A cave. A weekend. A box of scraps.

    That's how the first suit got built. No lab, no team of engineers, no second chances. MARK I is the same brief for you: one day to turn an idea into a working prototype that a room full of judges, mentors and recruiters can actually touch.

    Hosted by the GeeksforGeeks Student Chapter at Bennett University, Greater Noida, MARK I brings together 500 builders across AI, web, hardware and open innovation. Come with a team or get matched at check-in. Leave with a demo, a network and, if you're good, a very large cheque.
